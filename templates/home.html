About Us

Sewa Ghar

This appis to fix all problems related to your homes like home appliances, electricians, carpentry, and other household issues. In this app, Home Owners request the service from professionals and professionals will give the service to the home Owners. A mediator or admin can manage and view the activity of both homeowners and professionals. The aim of the project is to create Sewa Ghar to choose professionals by viewing their ratings, profile, etc.

For Home Owners

User Manual for Home Owner

  • Login or register to create an account on SewaGhar's home service provider web app.
  • Select the service you need from the list of available services or choose a service category that best fits your needs.
  • After selecting a service, choose a sub-service that is specific to your requirements.
  • Browse through the list of professionals who are qualified to provide the service you need and select the one that you prefer.
  • Take an appointment based on the professional's availability.
  • Once you have confirmed the appointment, you can book the service by providing details such as your location, date, and time of the appointment.
  • After booking the service, you can view your bookings to keep track of your appointments and make any necessary changes or cancellations.
  • Once the service has been completed to your satisfaction, you can pay the service charge through the app.

For Professionals

User Manual for professionals

  • Login or register to create an account on SewaGhar's home service provider web app.
  • View incoming requests from homeowners that match the services you provide.
  • Contact the homeowner to discuss the request, confirm details and schedule the service visit.
  • Go to the homeowner's location to provide the service that was requested.
  • After completing the service, let the homeowner know the total cost of the service, including any additional charges for parts or materials.
  • Wait for the homeowner to approve the total cost and then click the "Solve" button on the app to generate the payment.
  • Once the payment is approved, receive the payment through the app.
