Sewa Ghar

This appis to fix all problems related to your homes like home appliances, electricians, carpentry, and other household issues. In this app, Home Owners request the service from professionals and professionals will give the service to the home Owners. A mediator or admin can manage and view the activity of both homeowners and professionals. The aim of the project is to create Sewa Ghar to choose professionals by viewing their ratings, profile, etc.
