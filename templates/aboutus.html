About SewaGhar

Sewa Ghar

SewaGhar exists to fix all problems related to your home — home appliances, electrical work, carpentry, and other household issues. Home owners request a service from professionals, and professionals deliver that service right to their door.

A dedicated admin team manages and reviews the activity of both home owners and professionals, keeping the platform safe and reliable. Our aim is simple: help you choose the right professional by viewing their ratings, profile and experience — with zero guesswork.
